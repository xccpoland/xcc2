Our Story

Founded in 2026 after a breakthrough discovery involving a spare 12-inch driver, a pizza box, and a roll of industrial adhesive, harman/karton is disrupting the audiophile world. We boldly rejected heavy wooden cabinets and aluminum cones. Instead, we source only the finest, double-wall corrugated fiberboard to deliver a truly organic, paper-thin soundstage.
